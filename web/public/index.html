        About

        This static web app bundles VicFlora dichotomous and multi-access keys for offline use.

        On iOS, open this page in Safari, tap the share button, and choose “Add to Home Screen” to keep it available offline.

        On Android, open this page in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, and choose “Install app” (or “Add to Home screen”).
